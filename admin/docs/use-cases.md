# Use cases

| Situation | What to use |
|---|---|
| **Emergencies** | Medical and first-aid references, [Drug Reference](/docs/drug-reference), maps of your area and exit routes, emergency plans in the [Knowledge Base](/chat?knowledge_base=true) |
| **Teaching and study** | Kolibri courses (one account per learner), Wikipedia, textbooks from the [Library](/library), AI as a tutor |
| **Rural living** | How-to and farming guides, medical references, maps of your area |
| **Remote work** (sites, ships, research) | Technical manuals and procedures in the Knowledge Base, standards, note apps |
| **Travel** | Maps without roaming, Wikipedia in the destination's languages, health references |
| **Privacy** | Search, chat and documents stay on the server; nothing is sent to third parties |
| **Academic research** | Full Wikipedia, papers in the Knowledge Base, subject ZIM collections |

> Medical references are for information only and do not replace professional care.

## Building yours

1. **List the situations** in which you would be offline.
2. **Prioritise:** safety (medical, emergency) → main use → general reference (Wikipedia) → extras.
3. **Pick profiles** in [Easy Setup](/easy-setup): Essential to save disk, Comprehensive to cover everything.
4. **Add your own documents** to the Knowledge Base.
5. **Test before you need it.**

Need more? [Content Explorer](/settings/zim/remote-explorer) · [Content Manager](/settings/zim) · Kolibri channels.
