# About

**monolith-nomad** is an offline knowledge server in Brazilian Portuguese: a Nothing interface, a pt-BR catalog, a Library with PDF and EPUB readers, Brazilian maps and integrity-checked downloads.

## What sets it apart

- **pt-BR by default**: interface, docs, catalog, time zone and regional formats.
- **Smart downloads**: official Kiwix mirrors benchmarked first, aria2c with resume, SHA-256 checked before installing.
- **Library**: openly licensed works in PDF, EPUB and ZIM, by area, subject, language and level.
- **Brazilian maps**: the whole country, regions or states.
- **Own distribution**: installer, images, updates and catalogs come from this repository.
